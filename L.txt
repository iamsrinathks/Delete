**Side finding: with gh authentication off, `gh` doesn't start at all**
- The error is *"failed to read configuration: open …\GitHub CLI\config.yml: Access is denied"*.
- **My reading of this:** when `auth.gh` is on, the sandbox seems to give `gh` its placeholder token **and** read access to its config folder. When it's off, that folder is blocked (it's under your home folder, like FS-06). So `gh` **fails at startup**, even for commands that don't need sign-in.
- **Effect:** turning off gh authentication = **no gh in the sandbox at all**, not just "gh signed out". Fine for a locked-down policy, but worth noting in the user FAQ.
