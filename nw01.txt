**Why it matters for the rollout**
- One typo, a CIDR range or a URL in `allowedHosts` is enough to **silently run every session unsandboxed**.
  - Example: a developer adds `"10.0.0.0/8"` to reach an internal registry, the sandbox switches off, and they don't know.
- **Ring 1 cannot start until we prove that managed policy prevents this.** The test: Intune or server sets `sandbox.enabled: true`, the user file holds an invalid value, and the sandbox **stays on**.
